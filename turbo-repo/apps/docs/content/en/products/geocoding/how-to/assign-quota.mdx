# Assign an organization's quota

**For:** platform owners. **Setup:** ModularIoT.

Only provider calls count. Geofence, Plus Code, coordinates, internal addresses and cache use no quota.

<Steps>

### Check the plan

Open **Settings › Platform** and, in the **Geocoding** group at the end of the menu, choose **Plan**.

![Geocoding plan](/geocoding/platform-plan.png)

| Field | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| Apply the quotas | On: an organization without a subscription does not call the provider, and when its quota runs out, what its subscription says applies. Off: no call is refused, but usage is recorded and shown |
| Default monthly quota (calls) | Calls included for a subscription without its own quota |
| Alert at (% of the quota) | Percentage at which the alert is published, once per month |
| Monthly price (USD) | What each subscribed organization pays per month |
| Price per call over the quota (USD) | What each call over the quota costs, only with **Charge overage** |

A change applies to the whole current month. Closed months keep the terms they had at the close.

### Assign the quota

Choose **Organizations** in the same group and click **Assign quota**.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Search for it by name, identifier or RUT |
| Monthly quota | Empty: the plan's. A number: that organization's own quota |
| When the quota is used up | **Send to review**: stops calling and the places wait for review. **Charge overage**: keeps calling and charges each extra call. It applies only with **Apply the quotas** on |

To change it later, click the pencil on the row.

### Follow usage

The table shows each organization with a subscription or with usage in the month:

![Organizations with their month's usage](/geocoding/platform-organizations.png)

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Used / quota | The month's provider calls and the quota. Shows **plan's** when the organization uses the plan's quota |
| Cache | Percentage of resolutions answered by the cache |
| Provider cost | Sum of the cost per call of the month's calls. Only the platform owner sees it |
| Charge | Monthly price plus overage |
| When used up | What happens when the quota runs out |

The filters at the top choose the month, the quota status (normal, on alert, used up) and the mode.

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
The month is the calendar month in UTC. In mainland Chile, the geocoding month changes at 21:00 or 20:00 on the last day, depending on summer or winter time.
</Callout>

## What the organization sees

The organization owner sees its quota, its resolutions and its charge in **Settings › Trips › Monthly usage**. It does not see the provider's cost.

![An organization's monthly usage](/geocoding/org-monthly-usage.png)

## Through the API

| Action | Call |
| --- | --- |
| Change the plan | `PUT /api/v1/platform/geocoding-plan` |
| Assign or change the quota | `PUT /api/v1/orgs/{org}/geocoding-plan` with `monthlyQuota` and `onExhausted` |
| Usage of all organizations | `GET /api/v1/platform/geocoding-plan/usage?month=YYYY-MM` |

Details in [Plan, usage and provider API](/en/products/geocoding/reference/plan-api).
