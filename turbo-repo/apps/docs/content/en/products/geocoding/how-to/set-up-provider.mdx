# Set up the provider

**For:** platform owners. **Setup:** ModularIoT (the web application at modulariot.com/app).

The platform contracts a geocoding provider and pays for it. Each organization uses it through a connection that a platform owner creates. This guide gets the provider ready and connects an organization.

| Step | Where | Selector scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Store the provider's token | Settings › Credentials | **Platform** |
| 2. Choose the provider | Settings › Connections | **Platform** |
| 3. Connect the organization | Settings › Connections | The organization |
| 4. Give it a quota | Settings › Platform › Geocoding | Any |

You change the scope in the organization selector, at the top right. **Platform** is the first option in the list and only platform owners see it.

<Steps>

### Store the provider's token

1. Change the selector to **Platform**.
2. Open **Settings › Credentials** and click **Add credential**.
3. Search for `map` and choose **Mapbox** or **Google Maps**.
4. Paste the token and save.

![Mapbox and Google Maps credential types](/geocoding/credential-types.png)

Use a Mapbox server token, without URL restrictions, with permanent mode enabled in your Mapbox account. The token is not shown again.

### Choose the provider

1. With the selector on **Platform**, open **Settings › Connections**.
2. If the **Platform geocoding** card exists, click its three dots and **Edit**. If not, click **New connection** and choose **Geocoding · platform provider**.
3. Fill in the form and click **Save**.

![Platform geocoding provider form](/geocoding/platform-provider.png)

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Provider | **Mapbox** |
| Credential | The one from step 1 |
| Permanent mode | On. Without it, no provider result is used: all go to review |
| Our cost per call (USD) | What the provider charges per call in permanent mode, under its contract, for example `0.005`. It is copied into each usage row. It is not what the organization pays |

<Callout type="warning">
**Pitfall:** with **Google Maps** as the provider, each call is charged and the place goes to review, because the platform does not store Google results (default setting, pending a review of its terms). Use Mapbox with permanent mode.
</Callout>

The card can still show **Draft · Never tested** after saving. That status does not block calls. **Test** checks the token: with Mapbox, at no cost; with Google, it geocodes an address and the provider charges for it.

### Connect the organization

1. Change the selector to the organization.
2. Open **Settings › Connections** and click **New connection**.
3. Choose **Geocoding · platform provider**.

![New connection picker with the geocoding option](/geocoding/new-connection-picker.png)

The **Geocoding · ModularIoT** card appears, active:

![Geocoding · ModularIoT connection card in an organization](/geocoding/org-connection-card.png)

The connection stores no token: it uses the platform's. Repeating the step does not create another one. If the organization had another geocoding connection, it is deleted.

Without this connection, the organization still resolves by geofence, Plus Code, coordinates, internal addresses and cache. The only thing it does not do is call the provider: those places go to review with `geocoding: this organization has no geocoding connection; a platform owner adds it in Conexiones`.

To remove it, click its three dots and **Delete**.

### Give it a quota

If the quotas apply, the organization needs a subscription. Follow [Assign an organization's quota](/en/products/geocoding/how-to/assign-quota).

</Steps>

## Check

Resolve an address that is not in the internal addresses or in the cache (see [Your first location](/en/products/geocoding/tutorials/first-location)) and open **Settings › Trips › Monthly usage**. The **Quota** card must add one call. If the provider did not find the address, the call appears in the **None** or **Provider** row with the result **To review**.
