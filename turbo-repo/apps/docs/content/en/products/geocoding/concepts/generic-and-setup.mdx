# Generic and setup-specific

**For:** everyone.

Geocoding has a part that is the same for everyone and a part that is adjusted to each country and each customer. Knowing which is which tells you what to prepare when you add a customer or a country.

## The generic part

It is the same in every installation and is not configured per customer.

| Piece | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The source order | Geofence, Plus Code, coordinates, internal addresses, cache, provider ([details](/en/products/geocoding/tutorials/resolution-order)) |
| The district check | The point must fall in the declared district, with 500 m of tolerance |
| The choice of geofence | The one that contains the point, or a new circle |
| The cache | Shared by all organizations. It stores the normalized address, its district and its point; it does not store the organization, the place name or the customer |
| The quota and the usage record | Per organization and per month |
| The API contract | `POST /trips/locations/resolve`, the feed sites, the review |
| The review | To review, place by hand, reject |

## The specific part

| Piece | Depends on | Where it is defined |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Administrative boundaries | The country | Platform data. Today: Chile and Peru |
| Internal addresses | The country | Platform data. Today: Chile |
| The provider and its certification | The country | Platform connection; [Certify a new country](/en/products/geocoding/how-to/certify-a-country) |
| The country of each site | The feed | Each site carries `country`; the integration sets it per spreadsheet |
| Place types and their radii | The customer's business | Type of each location. The radii are fixed per type: port 1000 m, plant 500 m, distribution center 300 m, zone 200 m, customer 150 m |
| How the customer writes its places | The customer | The spreadsheet and the integration that reads it ([Send sites from a feed](/en/products/geocoding/how-to/feed-sites)) |
| The quota and what happens when it runs out | The contract with the customer | Settings › Platform › Geocoding |

## What to prepare

| When adding | Prepare |
| --- | --- |
| A customer in a country already loaded | Its geocoding connection, its quota, the type of each place and its spreadsheet columns |
| A new country | Its administrative boundaries, its address sources if they exist, and the provider certification |
| A new provider | Its adapter in the platform and a review of its terms (whether they allow storing the point) |
